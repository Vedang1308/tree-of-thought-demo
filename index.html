Experiment 1 · paper §4.1 and Figure 2

Game of 24, one search step at a time

Combine 4 numbers with + − × ÷ to reach 24. ToT takes 3 steps, one equation each. At every step the LM proposes equations, rates what is left, and only the best b states survive.

Simulated LM. Proposals and ratings are produced in your browser by rules that copy the paper's prompts (quick lookahead plus "too big / too small" common sense), with random sampling. No model is called, so it runs instantly and offline.
